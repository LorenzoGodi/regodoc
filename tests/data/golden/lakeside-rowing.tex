\RdSection{1}{rd-in-short}
{In short}
{In short}

The Lakeside Rowing Club keeps \RdMono{3,418} member records, a photo archive of
\RdMono{1,284.5~GB} and a shed with 8-seat boats; the survey took place on 21 August
2026.
\par
\begin{RdList}{numbered}
\RdItem Move the member records to one shared register (\RdRef{rd-members}{Part~I}).
\RdItem Repair the shed network before winter (\RdRef{rd-shed}{Parts~III–IV}).
\RdItem Keep two copies of the photo archive, one away from the lake (\RdRef{rd-archive}{Part~V}).
\end{RdList}
\begin{RdBox}{gray}{}

Already working well: the booking sheet for the boats and the weekly safety check.
\par
\end{RdBox}
